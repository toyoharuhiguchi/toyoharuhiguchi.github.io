The Survival-Breakdown Model: An Integrated Foundational Theory of Economic Agency

Author: Toyoharu Higuchi
Published: March 14, 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19020443

This paper presents the Survival-Breakdown Model (SBM) as an integrated foundational theory of economic agency. It argues that economic agency should be theorized not from optimization, but from prior conditions of constitution, survivability, basic feasibility, and continuation. On this basis, the paper develops a five-stage framework covering survival and breakdown, catastrophic shocks and resource decomposition, basic feasible slack and feasible action sets, the dynamics of continuation, destabilization, and termination, and the distinction among constitutive goods, stabilizing goods, and choice goods.

The paper further argues that the key difference from standard utility theory lies not in formal representability, but in theoretical order. The proposed order is: constitutive conditions, survivability, basic feasibility, continuation, and then optimization.
