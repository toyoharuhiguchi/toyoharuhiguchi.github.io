Research Papers

The Survival-Breakdown Model (SBM) is a foundational theory of economic agency in which constitutive conditions, survivability, basic feasibility, and continuation precede optimization.

This site is the official research page for the original three-paper core series in which SBM is formulated, developed, and theoretically positioned.

Important: In this research program, the Japanese texts are the authoritative originals, and the English texts are reference translations. For precise interpretation, citation, and theoretical verification, the Japanese versions should be treated as primary.

About

This page provides access to research papers by Toyoharu Higuchi, including abstract pages, PDF files, DOI information, Zenodo records, and author identification details.

Its central focus is the original three-paper core series of the Survival-Breakdown Model (SBM), presented as the foundational, developmental, and theoretical-positioning core of the SBM research program.

Original Formulation Note

Core Paper 1 establishes the foundational architecture of SBM.

Core Paper 2 develops SBM by reclassifying demand-supply theory, general equilibrium theory, and macroeconomics as conditional special cases rather than universal first principles.

Core Paper 3 organizes the main criticisms and clarifies SBM’s relation to standard theory as one of theoretical repositioning rather than simple opposition.
